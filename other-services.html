Our process

Our Recruitment Support is Tailored to Your Hiring Needs

01

Understanding Your Requirements

We understand the job role, responsibilities, required skills, experience and candidate expectations.

02

Candidate Sourcing

We search for potential candidates through relevant recruitment channels and our candidate network.

03

CV Screening & Shortlisting

We review candidate profiles against the requirements shared by your organization.

04

Initial Candidate Screening

We conduct initial screening to assess relevant experience, interest, availability and other role-specific requirements.

05

Interview Coordination

We coordinate interviews between shortlisted candidates and your hiring team.

06

Offer & Joining Follow-up

We follow up with selected candidates regarding offer acceptance and joining status.

07

Recruitment Updates

We maintain communication with your team regarding candidate progress and hiring status.
